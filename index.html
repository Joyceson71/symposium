A convergence of intellect, innovation, and ambition — where the sharpest engineering minds from across colleges compete, collaborate, and create. Whether you're debugging circuits at midnight or presenting research that rewrites the textbook, TechnoKings is your arena.

PAPER PRESENTATION
Ink Meets Innovation

Showcase your research and innovative ideas before a panel of expert judges. Present a technical paper on any topic in Electronics, Communication, or Emerging Technologies — from 5G mmWave to neuromorphic computing. Defend your data, impress the jury.

  • Team size: 1–2 members
  • IEEE-format report + PPT required
  • 10 min presentation + 5 min Q&A
  • Plagiarism must be below 20%

PROJECT EXPO
Build It. Show It. Own It.

Bring your hardware or software masterpiece to the expo floor. From IoT systems to ML-powered classifiers — the TechnoKings Project Expo is your stage. Connect your circuits, boot your code, and let your prototype speak louder than any slide deck.

  • Team size: 1–2 members
  • Working prototype strongly preferred
  • Judged: Innovation, Execution, Impact, Presentation

03
TECHNICAL
⚡
CIRCUIT BREAKERS
Fault Found. Victory Claimed.

You have a broken circuit. A ticking clock. And your bare hands. Circuit Breakers is the ultimate hands-on ECE battleground — identify faults, rewire connections, and build working circuits from scratch under tournament pressure.

  • Team size: 1–2 members
  • Components and tools provided on-spot
  • 3 rounds: Fault ID → Circuit Build → Speed
  • No phones or external datasheets

04
TECHNICAL
🧠
TECHNICAL QUIZ
Fast Buzzers. Sharp Minds.

From Maxwell's equations to modern microcontrollers — how deep does your ECE knowledge run? The TechnoKings Technical Quiz is a multi-round elimination battle covering core electronics, communication systems, semiconductors, and digital design.

  • Team size: 1–2 members
  • 4 rounds: Written → Rapid fire → Visual → Buzzer
  • Elimination after each round
  • Topics: Analog, Digital, EDC, Signals, VLSI, Comm

🕷️ NON-TECHNICAL EVENTS
⏱️
MINUTE TO WIN IT
60 Seconds of Chaos

Stack, balance, spin, and scramble — complete wild, prop-based challenges in under 60 seconds each. Simple rules. Impossible under pressure. The audience goes absolutely wild every single time.

  • Team size: 1–2 members
  • Multiple knockout rounds
  • All props provided on-site
🔍
DETECTIVE
The Clues Don't Lie. Can You?

A crime scene awaits. Evidence is scattered. Time is running out. Observe the scene, decode the clues, connect the dots, and name the culprit before other teams beat you to it.

  • Team size: 1–2 members
  • Points for correct culprit + fastest solve
  • Red herrings included — trust nothing
📦
BOX HUNT
Find the Box. Claim the Points.

Numbered boxes are hidden across the campus. Box Hunt is a full-campus scavenger race where your speed, observation, and navigation skills determine your destiny. The team that hunts hardest, wins hardest.

  • Team size: 1–2 members
  • 45-minute time limit
  • Each box contains a code to submit
🎵
START MUSIC
Name It Before the Drop

The song starts. You have 5 seconds. Three genres. Zero tolerance for slow reflexes. A buzzer-based rapid-fire showdown where your playlist knowledge becomes your competitive edge — Tamil, English, Bollywood, EDM, Wildcard.

  • Team size: 1–2 members
  • Buzzer system — first buzz answers
  • Wrong answer = negative points
